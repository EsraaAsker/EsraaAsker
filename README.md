## About

I am pursuing an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering, with research focused on AIoT-enabled rehabilitation, wearable sensing, and smart healthcare. My work spans applied machine learning, time-series analytics, computer vision, and image processing. I also bring **two years of programming instruction experience**, translating technical concepts into clear, hands-on learning.

## Technical focus

- **Computer vision and image processing:** OpenCV, image segmentation, contour detection, feature extraction, pattern recognition, and OCR workflows.
- **Machine learning:** classification, regression, clustering, anomaly detection, feature engineering, and model evaluation.
- **Wearable sensing:** EMG/IMU time-series, human activity recognition, rehabilitation-exercise assessment, and participant-independent evaluation.
- **Engineering:** Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, FastAPI, Streamlit, SQL, Git, and Linux.

## Selected projects

- **[AI Quality Inspection](https://github.com/EsraaAsker/ai-quality-inspection)** — CPU-compatible PatchCore-style anomaly detection for MVTec AD `metal_nut`, with validation-only threshold selection, defect heatmaps, a FastAPI endpoint, and a Streamlit inspection app. The audited run reports image AUROC **0.950** and pixel AUROC **0.905**.
- **[KneE-PAD Multimodal ML](https://github.com/EsraaAsker/knee-pad-multimodal-ml)** — CNN–Transformer workflows combining wearable EMG and IMU signals for exercise recognition and quality assessment.
- **[Time-Series Leakage Audit](https://github.com/EsraaAsker/time-series-leakage-audit)** — evaluation checks for overlapping-window leakage, train-only preprocessing, and validation/test separation.
- **[Evidence-Aware ML Evaluation](https://github.com/EsraaAsker/evidence-aware-ml-evaluation)** — connects experiment claims to protocols, metrics, artifacts, and limitations.
- **[Cross-Dataset Generalization](https://github.com/EsraaAsker/cross-dataset-generalization)** — dataset compatibility and generalization workflows involving KneE-PAD, GAITEX, and PHYTHMO.
- **[Rehabilitation ML Results Dashboard](https://github.com/EsraaAsker/rehabilitation-ml-dashboard)** — Streamlit dashboard for comparing model results and confusion matrices.
- **[OIBSIP Data Science Portfolio](https://github.com/EsraaAsker/OIBSIP)** — five applied projects covering EDA, classification, regression, and NLP.
